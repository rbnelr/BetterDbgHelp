Theory:

example addresses can benefit from caching between each other and when repeated
sweep can benefit from caching even more since neighbouring addresses have almost identical binary search
fuzzing cannot benefit from caching, but symbol data can be biased in address, thus making some search steps very likely, or entire symbol table can fit in certain cache levels

real world profiler usage may not fit with any of these, as real world is not a sweep, but also not completely uniform random
it's likely clustered around hotspot inner loops and thus somewhere between example and fuzzing
